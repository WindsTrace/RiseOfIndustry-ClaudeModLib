[h1]CityProgress — city upgrade progress[/h1]

A panel with every city's tier and how far its population is from the next tier, so you know where your deliveries pay off.

[b]Requires ClaudeModLib[/b] (listed under Required items; Steam offers to subscribe it too).

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]In a game, open the ClaudeModLib panel (gear button top-right, or [b]F9[/b]) and switch on [b]Show city progress window[/b]. Close it with the × in its title bar.
[*]Each card: population, tier → next tier, the city's own threshold for the next tier, how much is missing, and the status:
[list]
[*][b]Growing[/b] — shops are consuming your goods.
[*][b]Stagnant[/b] — not enough goods delivered.
[*][b]At limit · needs a sponsor[/b] — someone has to own the region permit before the city can upgrade.
[*][b]Upgrade contracts running[/b] — deliver the construction goods (Wall Panels, Bricks, Reinforced Walls).
[/list]
[*]Switch [b]Only cities I have supplied[/b] at the bottom of the window.
[/list]

[h2]How cities grow (from the game code)[/h2]
Every 15 days shops consume delivered goods; each consumed product adds population (higher-tier products add more, up to 5 products count). Each city rolls its own threshold per tier (Village 125k–175k, Town 275k–325k, City 475k–525k, Metropolis 725k–775k). At the threshold growth stops until the upgrade contracts are completed.

For Rise of Industry 2.3.x. Source: [url=https://github.com/WindsTrace/RiseOfIndustry-ClaudeModLib]github.com/WindsTrace/RiseOfIndustry-ClaudeModLib[/url]

[h1]CityProgress — 城市升级进度[/h1]

显示每座城市的当前等级，以及人口距离下一等级还差多少，让你知道往哪里送货最有效。

[b]需要 ClaudeModLib[/b]（已列为“必需物品”，订阅时 Steam 会提示一起订阅）。

[h2]使用方法[/h2]
[list]
[*]进入对局后打开 ClaudeModLib 面板（右上角齿轮按钮或 [b]F9[/b]），打开[b]“显示城市升级进度窗口”[/b]。点标题栏的 × 关闭。
[*]每张卡片显示：总人口、当前等级 → 下一等级、这座城市自己的下一级门槛、还差多少，以及状态：
[list]
[*][b]成长中[/b] —— 商店正在消耗你送的商品。
[*][b]停滞[/b] —— 送货不够。
[*][b]已封顶·等待赞助[/b] —— 需要有人持有该区许可证，城市才能升级。
[*][b]升级任务进行中[/b] —— 去送建材（墙板、砖块、加固墙）。
[/list]
[*]窗口底部可以切换[b]“只显示我供过货的城市”[/b]。
[/list]

[h2]城市如何成长（来自游戏代码）[/h2]
商店每 15 天消耗一次送到的商品，每种被消耗的商品都会增加人口（高级商品加得更多，每次最多计 5 种）。每座城市对每个等级会随机一个自己的门槛（村镇 12.5万–17.5万、城镇 27.5万–32.5万、城市 47.5万–52.5万、大都市 72.5万–77.5万）。到达门槛后人口停止增长，直到完成升级任务。

适用于《工业崛起》2.3.x。源码：[url=https://github.com/WindsTrace/RiseOfIndustry-ClaudeModLib]github.com/WindsTrace/RiseOfIndustry-ClaudeModLib[/url]
